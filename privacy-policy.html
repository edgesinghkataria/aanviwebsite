Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Aanvi Karmakar ("I", "me", or "my") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and protected when you visit this website (aanvikarmakar.com) or contact me through it.

Information I Collect

I collect only the minimum information necessary to respond to your inquiry:

  • Contact inquiries: When you email me directly, I receive your name, email address, and the content of your message.
  • Analytics data: This website may use privacy-respecting analytics to understand aggregate visitor patterns (e.g., which pages are viewed most). No personally identifiable data is collected through analytics.
  • Technical data: Standard server logs may capture your IP address and browser type. These are used only for security and performance purposes and are not shared.

How I Use Your Information

Information you provide is used solely to:

  • Respond to booking inquiries, casting requests, or general correspondence
  • Communicate with agents, casting directors, and industry professionals
  • Improve the performance and accessibility of this website

I do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Cookies

This website uses minimal cookies. Any cookies present are strictly functional or used for basic analytics. You can disable cookies in your browser settings without affecting your ability to view this website.

Third-Party Services

This website uses the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policies:

  • Google Fonts — for typography (Google Privacy Policy applies)
  • Image/media CDN — for serving portfolio images efficiently

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to any personal data I hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent for communications at any time

To exercise these rights, please contact me at hello@aanvikarmakar.com.

Data Retention

I retain contact correspondence only as long as necessary to fulfil the purpose of your inquiry. Analytics data is anonymised and retained in aggregate form only.

Changes to This Policy

I may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the website after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
