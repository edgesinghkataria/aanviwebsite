Accessibility
Statement

Last updated: May 2026

WCAG 2.1 Level AA — Target Standard

I am committed to making this portfolio website accessible to everyone, including people with disabilities. This statement outlines the steps taken and the current accessibility status of aanvikarmakar.com.

Our Commitment

This website aims to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA. These guidelines explain how to make web content more accessible to people with disabilities, including those who use assistive technologies such as screen readers, keyboard navigation, or voice control.

What We Have Done

  • Semantic HTML: Pages use meaningful, structured HTML5 elements (headings, landmarks, lists) to support screen reader navigation.
  • Alt text: All portfolio images include descriptive alternative text for screen reader users.
  • Keyboard navigation: All interactive elements — links, buttons, video controls — are reachable and operable via keyboard alone.
  • Focus indicators: Visible focus outlines are present on interactive elements.
  • Colour contrast: Text and background colour combinations meet or exceed WCAG AA contrast ratios.
  • Responsive design: The website is fully usable on mobile, tablet, and desktop screen sizes.
  • Skip navigation: The page structure allows assistive technologies to skip repetitive content.
  • Video accessibility: The showreel video includes player controls and is muted by default to avoid unexpected audio.
  • Reduced motion: Animations are subtle and do not rely on motion to convey information.
  • ARIA labels: Key sections and navigation elements include ARIA labels for screen reader context.

Known Limitations

While I strive for full accessibility, some limitations currently exist:

  • Video captions: The showreel video does not currently include closed captions or a transcript. I am working to address this.
  • Complex images: Some editorial photographs convey mood and artistic intent that may be difficult to fully describe in alt text.
  • Third-party embeds: Any future third-party embedded content may not meet the same accessibility standards as the core website.

Technical Approach

This website is built with:

  • Valid, semantic HTML5
  • CSS for visual presentation only — content and structure are not dependent on stylesheets
  • Vanilla JavaScript for progressive enhancement — core content is accessible without JavaScript
  • No reliance on auto-playing audio or flashing content

Feedback & Contact

If you experience any difficulty accessing content on this website, or if you notice an accessibility issue I have not addressed, please contact me directly. I take all feedback seriously and will work to resolve issues promptly.

Email: hello@aanvikarmakar.com

I aim to respond to accessibility queries within 5 business days.

Formal Complaints

If you are not satisfied with my response to an accessibility concern, you may wish to contact the relevant national accessibility body in your country for further guidance.

Review Schedule

This statement is reviewed and updated annually, or whenever significant changes are made to the website. The current review date is May 2026.
